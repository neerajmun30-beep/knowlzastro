### 2. House Significance & Meaning

#### Angular Houses (1, 4, 7, 10) - Strong Influences
**First House**: Foundation of personality, physical appearance, self-identity, life approach
**Fourth House**: Inner emotional security, home environment, mother, property, education completion
**Seventh House**: Partnerships, marriage, business relations, public dealings, spouse characteristics
**Tenth House**: Public image, career, reputation, authority, professional achievements, social status

#### Fixed Houses (2, 5, 8, 11) - Accumulating Influences
**Second House**: Financial resources, family, speech, food, material accumulation, early education
**Fifth House**: Creative expression, children, education quality, speculation, romance, intellect
**Eighth House**: Transformation, shared resources, occult knowledge, longevity, inheritance
**Eleventh House**: Gains, friendships, hopes, desires, elder siblings, social groups, income

#### Cadent Houses (3, 6, 9, 12) - Subtle Influences
**Third House**: Communication, siblings, courage, short journeys, writing, immediate environment
**Sixth House**: Health, service, enemies, obstacles, daily routine, work, debt, competition
**Ninth House**: Higher learning, philosophy, father, long journeys, dharma, spiritual path
**Twelfth House**: Spirituality, losses, foreign lands, expenses, liberation, subconscious, isolation

### 3. Transit Analysis Framework

#### Transit Impact Assessment
**Planet Strength**: Strong planets in transit create more significant effects
**House Position**: Angular house transits show immediate, visible effects
**Aspect Patterns**: Transiting planets aspecting natal planets activate their qualities
**Sign Compatibility**: Transits through friendly signs amplify positive effects
**Retrograde Influence**: Retrograde transits create internal, reflective periods
**Combustion Effects**: Planets too close to Sun lose some power temporarily

#### Transit Duration Significance
**Short-term** (Days to weeks): Emotional, mental adjustments, daily fluctuations
**Medium-term** (Weeks to months): Behavioral patterns, relationship dynamics, routine changes
**Long-term** (Months to years): Structural life changes, major developments, karmic shifts
**Extended** (Years to decades): Fundamental life transformations, spiritual evolution
