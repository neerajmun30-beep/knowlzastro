### 3. Transit Theory & Timing

#### Transit Principles
**Transit Mechanism**: When current planetary positions interact with natal chart positions
**Time-Based Effects**: Planetary transits activate natal potentials over specific time periods
**House Transits**: How planets moving through houses affect different life areas
**Sign Transits**: How planets in different signs modify their expression
**Conjunction Transits**: When transiting planets align with natal planets or sensitive points
**Aspect Transits**: Angular relationships between transiting and natal planets

#### Transit Duration & Intensity
- **Fast-moving planets** (Moon): Short-term emotional and mental influences (days to weeks)
- **Medium-speed planets** (Sun, Mercury, Venus, Mars): Medium-term influences (weeks to months)
- **Slow-moving planets** (Jupiter, Saturn): Long-term structural changes (months to years)
- **Outer planets** (Rahu, Ketu): Extended karmic patterns (years to decades)

#### Transit Effects by House Position
**Angular Houses** (1, 4, 7, 10): Strong, visible, immediate effects on life structure
**Fixed Houses** (2, 5, 8, 11): Steady, accumulating effects on resources and gains
**Cadent Houses** (3, 6, 9, 12): Subtle, internal effects on communication, service, and spirituality

### 5. Dasha System Theory

#### Dasha Concept
**Purpose**: Timing system revealing when planetary influences become active in life
**Mechanism**: Sequential periods ruled by planets based on nakshatra position at birth

#### Vimshottari Dasha
- **120-year cycle**: Complete rotation through all planetary periods
- **Period allocation**: Different durations for each planet based on traditional calculations
- **Sub-periods**: Each major period divided into sub-periods for detailed timing
- **Sub-sub-periods**: Further subdivisions for precise timing of events

#### Dasha Sequence
Starting from birth nakshatra, planets rule periods in specific order, each having unique characteristics and effects on life areas.

### 6. Yogas & Planetary Combinations

#### Yoga Concept
**Definition**: Specific planetary combinations that create significant effects in the chart
**Mechanism**: When planets form certain relationships, they produce specialized results

#### Yoga Categories
**Raj Yogas**: Combinations indicating leadership, authority, high status, success
**Dhana Yogas**: Wealth-producing combinations, financial prosperity, material abundance
**Sanyasa Yogas**: Spiritual combinations, renunciation, detachment, spiritual pursuits
**Chamatkara Yogas**: Surprising success combinations, unexpected good fortune
**Viparita Yogas**: Reversal patterns, success through adversity, turning challenges into strengths
**Parivartana Yogas**: Mutual exchange, planets exchanging houses for mutual benefit
**Neecha Bhanga**: Cancellation of debilitation, transforming weaknesses into strengths

### 7. Nakshatra Theory

#### Nakshatra Framework
**27 Nakshatras**: Division of zodiac into 27 equal segments, each approximately 13.33 degrees
**Lunar Connection**: Based on Moon's position at birth, indicating emotional and mental patterns
**Pada System**: Each nakshatra divided into 4 padas (quarters) revealing finer characteristics

#### Nakshatra Significance
- **Personality traits**: Basic character inclinations from birth nakshatra
- **Life patterns**: Recurring themes based on nakshatra placement
- **Compatibility**: Relationship dynamics through nakshatra matching
- **Remedial focus**: Specific remedies aligned with nakshatra qualities
- **Career inclinations**: Professional aptitudes indicated by nakshatra
